# Checkpoints

The trained weights of the revised paper (R3: Tables 1, 2 and 6) are archived on Zenodo:
DOI [10.5281/zenodo.23920498](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23920498) (CC BY 4.0). The record
contains 12 YOLO detectors, 4 fine-tuned BioCLIP heads, a model card with per-file test scores
and loading code, and `SHA256SUMS.txt` (`sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt`).

[`weights/paper/`](weights/paper/) keeps only the three R2 detector checkpoints (tag **v1.0.2**);
see that README.
